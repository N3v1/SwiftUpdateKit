# Code of Conduct
 
To build a truly great community, **SwiftUpdateKit** welcomes developers from all walks of life, with diverse backgrounds and a wide range of experience. A diverse and friendly community generates more great ideas, unique perspectives, and produces better code. We are committed to making the SwiftUpdateKit community welcoming to everyone.
 
To clarify what is expected of our members, SwiftUpdateKit’s Code of Conduct is based on the widely adopted [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/1/4/code-of-conduct/). This document articulates our values and expectations clearly.
 
### Contributor Code of Conduct v1.4
 
In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in SwiftUpdateKit and its community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
 
Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:
 
- Using welcoming and inclusive language (e.g., prefer non-gendered words like “folks” to “guys”, non-ableist words like “soundness check” to “sanity check”, etc.).  
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.  
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.  
- Focusing on what is best for the community.  
- Showing empathy towards other community members.
 
Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:
 
- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.  
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.  
- Public or private harassment.  
- Publishing others’ private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission.  
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.
 
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.
 
Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, documentation, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, or to temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviors deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
 
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces managed by SwiftUpdateKit, including (but not limited to) source code repositories, bug trackers, websites, documentation, and online forums. It also applies when an individual represents SwiftUpdateKit or its community in public spaces, such as through official project e-mail addresses, social media accounts, or as appointed representatives at events.
 
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the **SwiftUpdateKit maintainers** via the project’s communication channels or by flagging the behavior for moderation. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate. Maintainers are obligated to keep complaints confidential. The place where the behavior occurred is not the appropriate venue to discuss moderation decisions; moderators may remove such discussions.
 
Maintainers are held to a higher standard, and maintainers who fail to enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions determined by the project leadership. If you disagree with a moderation action, you may appeal privately to the project lead(s).
 
This policy is adapted from the Contributor Code of Conduct [version 1.4](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/1/4/code-of-conduct/).
